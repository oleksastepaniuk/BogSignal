A map of Polissia across Ukraine, Belarus, Poland and Russia, with our case-study sites Cheremske, Somyne and Syra Pohonia marked. A short explanation that some wetlands hide under forest, while drained ones now look like forest or farmland.

Why radar

A side-by-side or swipe comparison of the same site in optical (Sentinel-2) and radar imagery, with sensor and date under each. Three small cards explain what optical, Sentinel-1 C-band and NISAR L-band radar each "see".

Our experiment

A simple diagram of one U-Net model trained with four input sets: optical only, optical + Sentinel-1, optical + NISAR, and all sensors. Plain definitions of the outputs: "Existing wetland", "Restoration candidate" and "Needs review".

What we found

A table or chart comparing model accuracy for each input set, plus one local example where NISAR improved, didn't change or failed the prediction (marked "Illustrative" until real results arrive). Ends with an interactive map of one site with layer toggles and clickable findings.
