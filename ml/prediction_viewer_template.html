How this was made
  • The models were tested on {{NPATCH}} squares of 5.12 km inside one NISAR radar frame (track 100, frame 28), split into five test regions. Every square is drawn by the model that trained on the other four.
  • The map shows {{NBLOCK}} of those squares, {{KMW}} by {{KMH}} km: the block with no missing squares that holds the most wetland. It is drawn at the models' own 20 m detail, north up. Neighbouring squares can come from different models, so some seams show.
  • Existing maps are BogSignal's reference labels: older wetland, peat and land-cover maps combined. They are not ground truth.
  • Land types use a lowered bar for rare classes, set on the other four regions. Without it the baseline finds {{PLAIN_FOUND}} of wetland and is correct {{PLAIN_CORRECT}} of the time.
  • Found, correct and match count every labelled 20 m pixel. Match is the shared wetland area divided by the combined area. Ranking score is wetland average precision, the mean of the five regions.
  • The close-ups sit at the 95th, 75th, 50th, 25th and 5th percentile of match, averaged over the four models, among squares with at least 5% wetland and at most 5% unknown on existing maps.
